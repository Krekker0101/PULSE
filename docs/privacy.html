This Privacy Policy explains how PULSE (“PULSE,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may handle information when you use the service to discover and organize video content or manage publishing to connected platforms. The exact information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account and profile information: identifiers and basic profile details returned by a platform when you authorize a connection.
  • Authorization data: access tokens, refresh tokens, scopes, expiry information, and connection status needed to maintain an authorized integration.
  • Content and publishing details: video or post identifiers, URLs, captions, scheduling choices, publication status, and related metadata you choose to manage.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: logs, error details, and service events needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service.
  • Messages you send: information you provide when contacting the developer for support.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate connections, provide requested features, retrieve content metadata, organize content, carry out user-authorized publishing actions, show job status, troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations. We do not state that every listed feature is available on every platform.

3. Platform authorization and third parties

When you connect a third-party account, that platform may share information according to the permissions you approve. Your use of TikTok, Instagram, Telegram, YouTube, or another connected service is also governed by that service’s own privacy policy and terms. PULSE does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a platform when needed to perform an action you request, to infrastructure or service providers used to operate PULSE, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, security, and service integrity. Information is not shared for unrelated purposes unless permitted by law or you consent.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service to protect information. Authentication tokens should be stored securely and must not be shared publicly. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including operational, security, and legal needs. You can disconnect an integration through the relevant platform’s settings and revoke its authorization. To request deletion of information held by PULSE, contact the developer through the official project page or the support contact shown in the application. A dedicated privacy contact and a documented deletion process should be established before public launch.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. You may decline to connect an account, revoke permissions, or stop using the service. Some features may no longer work after access is revoked.

8. Children

PULSE is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law or the rules of connected platforms. We do not knowingly seek to collect children’s personal information in violation of applicable requirements.

9. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your own depending on the hosting and connected services used. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied to international transfers.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy by publishing a revised version on this page and changing the effective date. Material changes should be communicated where required by law.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the contact method provided by the PULSE developer in the application or its official project page. A dedicated privacy contact should be published before public launch.

This document is a general starting point and is not legal advice. Before launch, verify it against the actual data flows, vendors, retention settings, developer identity, and applicable privacy laws. Do not claim that a control or deletion process exists unless it has been implemented.
