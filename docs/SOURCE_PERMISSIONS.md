# Content rights and access are separate

Telegram **access** means the account/bot is legitimately permitted to read the post. **Republication rights** mean the video's owner permits redistribution, with separate consent for music/audio, edits and target platforms.

A public URL alone is NOT a license. New sources default to `unverified`; public discovery may operate where Telegram allows. External posting requires user to affirm their copyright/license basis and platform permissions. The state `user_attested` represents a **user declaration, not an independent legal verification**. False declarations remain the user's legal responsibility.

PULSE records: evidence text, platform permission flags and whether derivative edits are allowed. The current schema does NOT model license expiration or separate media-audio ownership. Independent evidence review, per-video rights overrides and license expiry enforcement are gaps listed in the audit; do not rely on this release for complex professional rights compliance.

Never use PULSE to bypass access controls, content protection, watermarks, or to republish content without appropriate rights. Deleting a source disables future intake and pending jobs but retains historical records for audits.
